Arcfuse – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Overview

Product Name: Arcfuse

Tagline: One Workspace. Every Community.

Vision:
Arcfuse connects conversations, communities, projects, and workflows from multiple social platforms into a single collaborative workspace.

Instead of switching between Discord, X, Reddit, Telegram, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Slack, users can manage discussions, collaborate with teams, discover opportunities, and build projects from one platform.

---

Problem Statement

Modern communities are fragmented.

A founder may receive:

- Feedback on Reddit
- Messages on X
- Feature requests on Discord
- Contributions on GitHub
- Updates in Slack
- Leads on LinkedIn

Managing these platforms separately creates:

- Context switching
- Lost opportunities
- Communication gaps
- Reduced productivity

Users need a unified collaboration layer across social platforms.
